# Data Collection Guidelines — Finance Forecasting

**Document classification:** INTERNAL  
**Owner:** Finance Operations

## Overview

This document defines the approved data sources and collection methodology for quarterly financial forecasts.

## Approved Data Sources

### Revenue Data
- CRM pipeline exports (Salesforce) — updated weekly
- Closed/won deal reports — updated daily at 06:00 UTC
- Renewal tracking spreadsheet — updated monthly by account management

### Cost Data
- ERP system (SAP) — source of truth for all actuals
- Budget templates — submitted by department heads quarterly
- Vendor contracts — Finance Ops maintains master list

## Data Quality Requirements

- All revenue inputs must be validated against the CRM within 48 hours of submission
- Actuals variance > 5% from prior period requires written explanation
- Exchange rates: use ECB daily rates as of the last business day of the period

## Collection Timeline

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| Week 1 | Department heads submit draft inputs |
| Week 2 | Finance validates and reconciles |
| Week 3 | Consolidated model reviewed by VP Finance |
| Week 4 | Final model presented to CFO |
