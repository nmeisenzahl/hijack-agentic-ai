# Reporting Standards — Financial Forecasts

**Document classification:** INTERNAL  
**Owner:** CFO Office

## Overview

All financial forecasts submitted for board and executive review must follow these standards.

## Required Sections

Every forecast package must include:

1. **Executive Summary** — One-page narrative with key assumptions
2. **Revenue Bridge** — Waterfall from prior period to current forecast
3. **Headcount Summary** — FTE count by department vs. budget
4. **Risk Register** — Top 5 risks with quantified impact and mitigation
5. **Scenario Analysis** — Base, upside (+15%), and downside (−20%) cases

## Formatting Requirements

- All currency in USD unless otherwise specified
- Rounding: millions to one decimal place (e.g., $142.3M)
- Variance: show both absolute ($) and percentage (%)
- Negative numbers in parentheses: (5.2M), not −5.2M

## Approval Workflow

| Forecast Type | Approver | SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly flash | Finance Manager | 24 hours |
| Monthly close | VP Finance | 3 business days |
| Quarterly board pack | CFO | 5 business days |
| Annual plan | CFO + CEO | 10 business days |
